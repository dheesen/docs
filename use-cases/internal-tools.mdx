Many businesses run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, email threads, and generic SaaS tools that almost fit but never quite do. Every workaround costs your team time and introduces the risk of errors. Azotha lets you build custom internal tools that match exactly how your team works — no compromise, no adapter layer, no developer required.

## Examples of Internal Tools You Can Build

Tell Azotha what problem you're trying to solve, and the agent will build a tool shaped around your actual process. Common internal tools include:

- **Order or inventory management dashboard** — track stock levels, orders, and fulfilment status in one place
- **Custom CRM or contact management view** — manage leads, customers, and follow-ups the way your sales process actually works
- **Internal reporting and KPI dashboard** — surface the numbers your team checks every day, pulled from your data sources
- **Team task tracker or project board** — assign work, track progress, and flag blockers without paying for seats in a tool designed for someone else
- **Quote or proposal generator** — turn inputs from your team into formatted, consistent quotes in seconds
- **Data import/export tool** — move data between systems, reformat files, and eliminate manual copy-paste work

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the tool and the problem it solves">
    Write a plain-language description of what the tool should do and which pain point it addresses for your team. You don't need technical specifications — explain it the same way you'd explain it to a new hire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the working prototype">
    The agent builds a working prototype and makes it accessible at your dedicated preview URL. Your team can click through the actual interface, enter real data, and see how the tool behaves — not a mock-up, but running software.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine and deploy">
    Collect feedback from the people who will use it daily, describe the changes you want, and Azotha updates the tool accordingly. Once you're satisfied, Azotha deploys the final version to your managed environment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Custom Over Off-the-Shelf

Off-the-shelf tools are built for the broadest possible audience, which means they come with assumptions baked in: their data model, their terminology, their workflow. Adopting them requires your team to adapt to the tool — renaming concepts, ignoring features that don't apply, and building manual bridges between the gaps.

A custom internal tool built with Azotha starts from your workflow, your data, and your terminology. There are no unused features cluttering the interface and no processes your team has to reshape to fit a vendor's opinion of how work should happen. When your process changes, you describe the change and the tool changes with it.

<Tip>
  Start with the most painful manual process in your business — the one your team most frequently works around, exports to a spreadsheet to fix, or flags as "we really need a better way to do this." That's your best first internal tool.
</Tip>
