Your clients shouldn't have to send emails to check on their project status, hunt for documents in shared drives, or wait for a reply to get basic information. A client portal gives them a dedicated, branded web application to interact with your business on their schedule — and Azotha builds it from your plain-language description, no development team required.

## What Is a Client Portal?

A client portal is a web application your customers log into to interact directly with your business. Rather than routing every request through email or phone, clients get a self-service interface tailored to your service. Depending on your business, a portal might let clients:

- View the status of open projects, orders, or service requests
- Access and download invoices, deliverables, or signed documents
- Submit new requests, questions, or support tickets
- Review and approve proposals or quotes
- See a history of their activity and communications with your team

## Example Portal Features

When you describe your portal to Azotha, you can include any combination of features that fits your client relationship. Common building blocks include:

- **Login and account management** — secure, per-client accounts with password management and session handling
- **Project or order status view** — a real-time or regularly updated view of where things stand
- **Document upload and download** — clients upload what you need from them; you share what they need from you
- **Communication or messaging thread** — a structured way to exchange messages tied to a specific project or request
- **Invoice or payment overview** — a clear summary of what's been billed, what's outstanding, and what's been paid

## From Idea to Live Portal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the portal and your customers' needs">
    Explain who your clients are, what they need to access, and what actions they should be able to take. Include any branding preferences — colours, logo, tone — and describe the data or systems the portal should connect to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the prototype at your preview URL">
    The agent builds a working prototype of the portal and makes it available at your dedicated preview URL. You can log in, navigate the interface, and experience it exactly as your clients will.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with a trusted client for early feedback">
    Before a full launch, share the preview URL with one or two trusted clients. Their real-world feedback will surface things no internal review can catch — unclear labels, missing information, or workflow gaps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and manage the live portal">
    Once you're confident in the experience, Azotha deploys and manages the live portal in your hosted environment. When your business changes, describe the update and the agent applies it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
