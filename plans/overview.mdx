Azotha is designed to grow with you. Start with **Core** to move fast — turn ideas into working software without any technical team. Step up to **Control** when your team is building regularly and you need automated security and reliability built in. Choose **Autonomous** when your software will reach real customers or external users and you need maximum assurance, compliance, and active technical guidance before anything goes live.

## Azotha Core — €1,000/mo <sup>excl. VAT</sup>

Core is your starting point. Get a managed AI-agent workspace, describe what you want in plain language, and watch your idea become a working prototype — often within hours. Core is ideal if you are exploring what AI agents can do for your business, want to make a software idea tangible quickly, and do not yet have a fixed technical process in place.

## Which Plan Is Right for You?

If you are just getting started with AI-powered software development, **Core** gets you moving without any infrastructure overhead. If your team ships internal tools on a regular cadence and downtime or security gaps would cause real problems, **Control** gives you automated protection with minimal effort. If your software will be used by customers, partners, or anyone outside your organisation, **Autonomous** provides the governance, compliance checks, and expert oversight you need before anything goes live.

Not sure where you fit? Azotha can help you decide after a brief conversation about your goals, timeline, and the type of software you want to build. Reach out and a member of the team will point you in the right direction.
