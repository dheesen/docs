## How It Works

Follow these three steps to go from a software idea to a live, working product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe Your Idea">
    Tell your agent what you want to build in plain language — no technical jargon, no specifications document required. The more clearly you describe your goal and your business context, the faster the agent can get to work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Agent Builds and Previews">
    The agent works inside your managed Azotha workspace, writing code, setting up workflows, and running tests. Results appear at your dedicated preview URL so you can see exactly what has been built before anything goes live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, Refine, and Go Live">
    Open your preview URL, review the output, and give feedback in plain language. Once you are satisfied with the result, Azotha handles deployment — your software goes live with no infrastructure work on your end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Azotha is fully managed. You never need to install software, configure a server, or manage infrastructure. Everything runs in your dedicated hosted workspace from day one.
</Note>
