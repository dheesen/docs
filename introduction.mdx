Azotha is a managed AI-agent platform that gives your business a dedicated workspace where AI agents design, build, test, and maintain real software on your behalf. You communicate in plain language — describing features, workflows, and business goals — and your agent translates those instructions into working applications, all hosted and managed by Azotha. No developers, no servers, no technical overhead.

## The Problem It Solves

Most small and medium-sized businesses have no shortage of software ideas. You might want an internal dashboard to track operations, a client portal to streamline communication, or an automated workflow to eliminate repetitive manual tasks. The barrier is rarely imagination — it is execution.

Hiring a development team is expensive, slow to ramp up, and hard to manage without a technical background. Freelancers introduce project risk and knowledge gaps. Off-the-shelf tools rarely fit your exact process and often require months of configuration. And while modern AI chat tools can help you think through a problem, they stop at conversation — they do not build and run software in a real environment where you can actually see and use the result.

Azotha closes that gap. Your agent does not just suggest — it builds.

## How Azotha Is Different

Most AI tools operate outside your actual workflow. Azotha puts the agent inside a managed hosted environment that is dedicated to your project, so every instruction you give produces a real, testable result.

- **Managed environment** — Azotha provisions and maintains your workspace. You never configure infrastructure or install anything locally. Your agent has everything it needs to build, run, and test software from the moment you start.
- **Real prototypes, not mockups** — your agent writes functional code and assembles working applications. You can open your preview URL and interact with actual software, not a slide deck or wireframe.
- **Persistent project context** — the agent retains your business logic, naming conventions, and the decisions you have made across every session. You do not re-explain your business every time you open a conversation.
- **Plain-language control** — you direct the agent the way you would brief a colleague. Describe the outcome you want, give feedback on what you see, and the agent iterates until the result is right.

## Who It Is For

Azotha is designed for entrepreneurs and business owners at small and medium-sized companies who want to move fast on software without building or managing a technical team.

You are a good fit for Azotha if you want to:

- **Automate workflows** — eliminate manual, repetitive processes by building automations that connect your tools and data.
- **Build internal tools** — create dashboards, reporting systems, and admin panels tailored to exactly how your team works.
- **Launch client portals** — give your customers a polished, branded experience for accessing information, submitting requests, or tracking progress.
- **Develop and validate a prototype** — test a product idea quickly with a working proof of concept before committing to a full build.

You do not need a technical background to get value from Azotha. If you can describe what you want your software to do, your agent can build it.
