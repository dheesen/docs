Azotha turns the way businesses have traditionally built software on its head. Instead of hiring a team, writing specifications, managing sprints, and waiting months for a result, you describe what you need and your AI agent gets to work immediately inside a fully managed, hosted environment. Here is a detailed look at each stage of that process.

## Your Managed Workspace

When you join Azotha, the team provisions a dedicated hosted workspace for your business. This environment contains everything your agent needs to build, run, and test software — compute, storage, network configuration, and tooling — all managed by Azotha on your behalf.

You never install software on your own machine or configure a server. Your workspace is accessible through a browser, and your agent is ready to receive instructions from day one. Azotha keeps the environment updated, secure, and running so you can focus entirely on what you want to build.

## Plain-Language Instructions

You communicate with your agent the same way you would brief any member of your team — in plain language. Describe the feature you want, the problem you are trying to solve, or the change you need, and the agent interprets your intent and determines the best way to implement it.

You do not need to know which programming language is appropriate, how a database should be structured, or how to configure a server. If you can articulate your business goal, the agent can handle the technical path to reach it. The more clearly you describe the outcome — including any business rules, data involved, or constraints — the more accurately the agent delivers what you have in mind.

## Building and Testing

Once it receives your instruction, the agent writes code, assembles components, configures integrations, and runs automated tests — all inside your managed workspace. This is not a simulation or a mockup: the agent is building real, functional software in a live environment.

As the agent works, it checks its own output, catches errors, and iterates to resolve them before surfacing the result to you. You track progress and see the finished output at your preview URL, where you can interact with the software directly and verify it behaves as expected.

## Persistent Context

Your agent retains a complete memory of your project across every session. It remembers the decisions you have made, the business logic you have explained, the naming conventions your team uses, and the feedback you have given on previous builds.

This means you never have to re-introduce your business or re-explain a past decision when you come back with a new task. The agent picks up exactly where you left off and applies accumulated context to every new instruction — so the software it builds becomes more aligned with your business over time, not less.

## AI Model Routes

Azotha connects your agent to 75+ AI model routes across leading providers — including OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex AI, Amazon Bedrock, Groq, DeepSeek, Ollama, and OpenRouter. You do not configure or manage any of these integrations yourself. Azotha selects the appropriate model route for each task based on what your agent is doing, ensuring you always get capable, cost-effective results without having to evaluate model choices on your own.

If your business has data-residency or compliance requirements, Azotha offers European-only model routes that guarantee your data and prompts are processed exclusively within European infrastructure. Ask the team about European routing when setting up your workspace.

## Going Live

When you have reviewed the preview and are satisfied with the result, Azotha handles the transition from preview to production. The team manages hosting, access controls, custom domains, and any final deployment steps. Your software goes live as a real, accessible application — without you touching any infrastructure.

For ongoing maintenance, updates, and new features, you continue directing the agent in exactly the same way. Your workspace persists, your context is preserved, and your software evolves alongside your business.
